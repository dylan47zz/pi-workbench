# Workbench Subscriptions

Read `_system/AGENTS.md`, then inspect the requested subset of `raw/subscriptions/` through `workbench-triage`.

For each item choose one outcome:

- retain as background;
- compile evidence or a synthesis update in `wiki/`;
- add a concrete next action to an active project;
- propose an `output/content` idea with a distinct personal claim;
- ignore for now.

Rank by relevance to active projects, long-running themes, contradiction with existing judgment, and potential to change a future decision. Do not summarize every article and do not delete the source.
